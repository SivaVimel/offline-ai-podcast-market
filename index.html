How it works.

A whole studio runs on the phone in your hand.

01

Choose a topic

Tap a featured card, browse Recent, or type your own search. Ten fresh angles appear every time.

02

The model writes

A bundled Llama 3.2 1B language model drafts the episode on-device, streaming each word as it arrives.

03

The voice speaks

Sentences are handed to the neural voice engine and spoken aloud in real time. No buffering, no servers.

Private by design.

Nothing you type, hear, or generate ever leaves your iPhone. Every word is written and spoken locally — the app works in airplane mode.
